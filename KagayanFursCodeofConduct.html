Every member is given the right to the freedom of speech and expression in accordance with our constitution. 

With this privilege comes the responsibility to recognize, accept, and comply with all rules, policies, and requirements concerning their role as a member of Kagay-an Furs as written in this document.

Server/Group Chat Rules

Members of Kagay-an Furs Messenger, Discord, and Telegram server/group chat are expected to follow the following:

  1. All are welcome to join, except for those who come with the sole purpose of harassment or hate.
  2. Hate speech of any kind is not tolerated. No harassment of other members regardless of their race, gender, sexuality, or religion.
  3. Minors are allowed across all platforms with the strict condition that they must be 16+, and it's important to be respectful to the minor and mindful of what you say.
  4. Keep adult conversations in the adult areas. This is a zero-tolerance offense and a non-negotiable rule.
  5. Do NOT prey on nor harass people; this is not your dating server/group chat. It is a social safe space.
  6. Do not argue with admins, mods, or the owner publicly. If you have an issue with how something was handled, privately message the issuer and handle it like a normal human being.
  7. Slurs and general use of hate speech are prohibited. Such examples of slurs are the N-word, R-word, etc.
  8. Interpersonal drama with other members is to remain outside of the server. Playful, unserious banter is perfectly fine; however, making serious jabs at members is unwelcome.
  9. Do not mention anything regarding self-injurious behaviors or habits. If you are psychologically unwell, seek professionally trained people who can help you. We are not your therapist, and it is a very sensitive topic that may trigger episodic relapses in some members.
  10. Alt accounts are strictly prohibited unless valid reason is stated. This is to de-escalate potential drama amongst members.
  11. Any form of venting is strictly to be expressed only in dedicated channels/group chats made for that purpose.
  12. Swearing of any kind is generally allowed but police yourself when doing so, Excessive swearing is still prohibited.

Dress Code Policy

To ensure that our meetups remain a safe and welcoming for all attendees and the general public, the following guidelines must be followed:

  1. Attendees must wear appropriate clothing. Wearing attire that would be considered inappropriate in a social setting is prohibited.
  2. Fursuiting, whether partial or full, is not a requirement but highly encouraged.
  3. Exposed bondage gear, pup masks, or any accessories with sexual connotations is strictly prohibited to ensure the meetup remains a safe space for all ages.
  4. A con badge must be worn during the entirety of the meetup. The con badge carries the name/reputation of the community. Therefore, you must act in accord with societal norms.
